Before there was an us…

There was just a random project, one late arrival, and a girl I had no idea I would come to love this much.

Hey, Birthday Girl.

I could tell you our whole story right here, but I think it deserves to be discovered, not handed to you all at once.

So I've tucked it into little chapters. Some will make you laugh. Some might make you say, "Wait, you still remember that?" And a few might remind you of moments you never realized meant so much to me.

You don't have to get every answer right. This isn't an exam, madam. You've had enough of those. It's just my slightly ridiculous, very sentimental way of taking you back through the little things that made us “us”.

Some memories are easy to capture in photographs. Others live in conversations, silly little moments, and things that probably wouldn't mean much to anyone else. But somehow, they're the things I find myself holding on to.

I won't give everything away just yet. You'll have to explore, remember, laugh at me when I deserve it, and maybe discover a few things you didn't know I'd kept close to my heart.

I wish I could be beside you today. Since I can't, I put a few pieces of us here — some funny, some little, some I don't think I'll ever stop remembering. The questions are just an excuse to get you to the memories.
